## Modelling scope and known limits

- Rectangular zones; partitions and inter-storey slabs are single interface stacks in a gap inserted at the shared plane, so zone sizes and surface areas equal the contract exactly. Junction strips/columns belonging to no surface are adiabatic (the RC model also ignores junction bridging).
- Windows/doors: interior film + orthotropic core matching the contract U; window solar enters as heat flux on the zone's floor slab, as in the validated single-zone model.
- Excluded on both sides: heating, infiltration, door-opening and stair airflow, opaque-surface solar, long-wave sky exchange. MAPDL here validates conduction through the envelope, partitions and slabs, not indoor airflow (no CFD claim).
- ANSYS air is a high-conductivity solid per zone (near-isothermal node); the reported ANSYS zone temperature is its volume-weighted mean.
- Exterior convection on ANSYS's opaque wall/roof faces uses a named, bounded, wind-speed-dependent film coefficient (McAdams 1954 simple combined: h = 5.7 + 3.8*V, 5-50 W/m2K, no windward/sheltered split since the weather source carries no wind direction). Openings and ground floors, and the whole RC side, keep the static contract h_outside (RC has no per-hour U input) - a disclosed, ANSYS-only refinement and an additional source of the gap below.
- The RC comparison uses multiroom_rc's lumped node with the `EFFECTIVE_MASS_FRACTION` capacitance convention; differences therefore include that modelling choice, which is what this validation is meant to expose.
- The legacy `benchmarks/ansys_reference` package is quarantined (`ansys-pipeline/quarantined/QUARANTINE.md`) and is not evidence.
