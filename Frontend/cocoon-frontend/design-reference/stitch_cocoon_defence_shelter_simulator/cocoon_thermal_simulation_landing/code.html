SIH 2026 Defence Innovation Technical Brief

Methodology Overview: Standard numerical methods for sub-zero shelter design require 6 to 12 hours per simulation on high-end HPC nodes, making real-time generative space layout impossible during operational deployment.

Our hybrid framework couples a 1D lump-parameter Resistive-Capacitive (RC) network with a Deep Operator Network (DeepONet) trained on high-fidelity ANSYS Fluent conjugate heat transfer simulations. The result is instant parametric updates within ±0.8°C of 3D FEM solvers across multi-layer envelopes.
